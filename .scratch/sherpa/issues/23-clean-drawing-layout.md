# 23: Drawings that do not overlap or run off the screen

**What to build:** the owner saw labels clipped at the screen edge, arrows dragged across the whole table, old marks piling up under new ones, text boxes on top of each other and a caption bar over the taskbar.

**Blocked by:** 20.

**Status:** built; owner check on screen pending.

- [x] Captions are pills (dark, outlined in the shape's colour) placed by a placer that keeps them on the screen, off the text and controls of the screen and off each other; it tries neat spots, then further out, then slid spots, then the one that covers least (render/place.ts, tests).
- [x] An arrow that points at a region gets its tail from the app: a short distance away in a free direction with a clear line (the model's own tail is ignored), so arrows are short and cross nothing. An arrow that repeats a box on the same thing is left out.
- [x] Pointing marks (box, ellipse, arrow, highlight, label, step number) are taken off when the next step draws something (unless `keep`), in the backend; squares, polygons and equations build up.
- [x] Squares on lines are true to size (a small overhang of the screen edge is allowed); the proof diagram is placed beside the figure, level with its top, and equations keep clear of it.
- [x] Boxes on text get a little room; text regions are lines of their own unless packed like a paragraph (so a menu item can be pointed at; evaluation still 31/31 and 30/31 on the two models in use, pointing scored over everything drawn in the turn).
- [x] The proof diagram uses the learner's own letter (x) for the hypotenuse.
- [ ] Known rough edges: a square built on a side of a big figure still covers what is under it (it is translucent); pills may touch text when the screen is very dense.
- [x] Small drawings (owner: the Pythagoras triangle stopped being drawn with its lines). Cause, found by shrinking the real video frame: a triangle smaller than about half its size was not recognised as a figure (a minimum share of the screen), because the text finder also took the small triangle with its labels for a block of text, and the filters against edge halos around text then dropped it, so there were no line regions to highlight. Now a small blob of edges is kept as a figure when its structure is a line drawing (at least three long straight segments that meet, one of them slanted, filling the box); such a drawing skips the text-halo filters; and the figures that get their sides found are chosen drawings first (browser bars and strips used to take the two places). Found down to about 0.4 of the original size on the real frame. 5 tests (a synthetic triangle at four sizes; small buttons and straight frames give no drawing). Evaluation unchanged (31/31 and 30/31).
- [x] The frame that went wrong for the owner (2026-10-04: a MathHelp lecture on a white board, a thin black triangle, handwriting, a presenter; saved as a private test image, not in the repo). Found: (1) the blob finder merged the triangle's strokes with the title, the video frame and the presenter into one blob the size of the screen, which was rejected, so no figure and no sides; only the JPEG the app really sends (compression breaks a thin stroke into pieces more than 20 px apart) made it worse than the PNG. Added closed_shape_regions: three or four straight segments whose ends meet in a ring, at least one slanted, found from the segments themselves, with tolerant joining; a frame-sized figure no longer hides the shapes inside it. (2) The proof diagram was put over the presenter's face, then over the title and the browser bar. Added image regions (big areas with many in-between tones: photos and video of people) and an occupancy map (hidden `detail` regions from the pixels: any text, handwriting, edge or photo; not shown to the model, not drawn) so the diagram goes on flat background; a bigger diagram far away loses to a smaller one close by; room is kept around the picture it is about. Checked live: sides highlighted (PNG and JPEG q92, q70), diagram on the clean part of the board. Tests: 209 backend (a synthetic lecture frame with presenter in three encodings; image regions; diagram off presenter and picture). Evaluation: DeepSeek 31/31; Qwen 30/31 pointed, keyword varies 7 to 10 of 12 between runs.
